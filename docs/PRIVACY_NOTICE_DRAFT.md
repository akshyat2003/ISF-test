## Startup Jathara platform: how we use your personal data

**Who we are.** The Startup Jathara platform is operated for Startup Jathara @ Dandakaranya, organised by GIET University in association with the International Startup Foundation. The Data Fiduciary responsible for your data is **[legal entity name and address]**. Zeny2Labs builds and operates the platform on its behalf.

**Contact and grievances.** **[Grievance officer name, email and phone]**. We reply within **[period set by Legal]**.

**Age.** The platform is for people aged 18 or over. When you register you confirm that you are 18 or older.

### What we collect and why

| Data | Why we use it |
|---|---|
| Your name, email address and/or mobile number | To sign you in with a one-time code, recognise you on WhatsApp, and contact you about your application or the event |
| Your startup's details and your answers on the application form | To review your application for the year(s) you chose |
| Selection decisions and their release | To tell you the result and give selected teams access to that year's event |
| Event participation (role, assignments such as your volunteer or assigned startups) | To run the event |
| Help Desk conversations and support tickets, including emergencies | To answer questions, resolve problems and respond to emergencies |
| Security and audit records (who did what, and when) | To keep the platform secure and accountable |

We do not ask for passwords. You sign in with one-time codes, and staff also use an authenticator app.

### Use of AI

- An AI system helps the Management Team by ranking applications against criteria that Management sets. **It never decides.** People in the Management Team make every selection decision.
- Your contact details are not sent to the ranking system.
- AI scores and explanations are used only internally.
- A separate AI Help Desk answers questions using official event information and only your own data that you are allowed to see.

### How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Applications that are not selected | 6 months after that year's event |
| Selected applications and decisions | 2 years |
| Accounts | 6 months after your last event |
| Help Desk conversations | 90 days after the event |
| Support tickets | 1 year |
| Security records | At least 1 year |

After that we delete or anonymise the data, unless the law requires us to keep it.

### Your rights

You can:
- ask for a summary of your data;
- ask us to correct or update it;
- ask us to erase it;
- withdraw your consent;
- nominate someone to act for you.

To do any of these, contact the grievance officer above. Withdrawing consent or erasing your data may mean you can no longer take part. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.
